# Vocabulary that moves results

Concrete, physical words steer image models; vague praise ("beautiful, 8k, masterpiece") does
not. Pick one term per row of the brief and describe it in plain words when a model ignores the
jargon. Describe looks by their properties, not by brand, product or artist names.

## 1. Framing

| Term | Frame |
|---|---|
| extreme close-up (ECU) | a single detail: an eye, a watch crown, a texture |
| close-up (CU) | face, or the product filling the frame |
| medium close-up (MCU) | head and shoulders, chest up |
| medium shot (MS) | waist up |
| medium long / cowboy | mid-thigh up |
| full shot (FS) | whole body or object, small margin |
| wide / establishing | subject small in its environment |
| flat lay / top-down | camera straight down at objects on a surface |

| Angle | Effect |
|---|---|
| eye level | neutral, documentary |
| low angle | subject looks powerful, monumental |
| high angle | subject looks small, overview |
| overhead (90°) | graphic, map-like arrangement |
| three-quarter view | product standard: front and one side visible |
| Dutch tilt | tension, unease (use rarely) |
| over-the-shoulder | viewpoint of a person in the scene |

Composition words: rule of thirds, centred symmetry, leading lines, frame within a frame,
negative space on the [left], foreground anchor, layered depth (foreground, midground,
background), horizon on the lower third.

## 2. Lens and aperture (full-frame equivalents)

| Focal length | Look |
|---|---|
| 14-24 mm | expansive space, stretched edges, strong perspective |
| 28-35 mm | environmental portraits, reportage, interiors |
| 50 mm | natural perspective close to the eye |
| 85 mm | flattering portrait compression, soft background |
| 100 mm macro | life-size detail, very shallow focus |
| 135-200 mm | compressed distance, isolated subject, smooth background |

| Aperture | Look |
|---|---|
| f/1.2-2 | very shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, one plane sharp |
| f/2.8-4 | subject sharp, background clearly soft |
| f/5.6-8 | most of the subject sharp; product and group shots |
| f/11-16 | front-to-back sharpness; landscapes, architecture; star-shaped highlights |

Also: "tilt-shift miniature look" (narrow focus band), "long exposure, motion-blurred water or
traffic trails", "high shutter speed, frozen splash", "slight film grain", "anamorphic
horizontal flares".

## 3. Lighting setups

| Setup | Write |
|---|---|
| Rembrandt | key 45° to the side and above; small triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek |
| loop | key slightly off-axis and above; small nose shadow toward the cheek |
| butterfly / Paramount | key high, directly in front; butterfly-shaped shadow under the nose |
| split | key at 90°; one half of the face lit, the other in shadow |
| broad / short | key lights the side of the face toward / away from the camera |
| clamshell | key above and a reflector below; even beauty light |
| rim / back light | source behind the subject outlining hair and edges |
| softbox key + fill card | large soft source on one side, white bounce card on the other |
| gradient sweep | a strip light grazing a seamless backdrop for a soft vignette |
| practical / motivated | visible lamps, screens or windows in the scene explain the light |
| high-key | bright, low contrast, few shadows |
| low-key | dark, high contrast, deep shadows, small lit areas |

Modifiers and qualities: softbox, octabox, beauty dish, umbrella, strip box, grid (narrow
beam), flag (blocks spill), diffusion scrim, bounce card; hard light (crisp shadows, specular
highlights) versus soft light (gradual falloff); light falloff across the background.

## 4. Time, weather and colour temperature

| Condition | Light |
|---|---|
| golden hour | low warm sun, long shadows, glowing rim on edges |
| blue hour | cool twilight, city lights warm against a blue sky |
| overcast | soft, shadowless, muted saturation |
| midday sun | hard, short shadows, high contrast |
| fog or haze | low contrast, depth fades with distance |
| rain at night | wet reflective surfaces, coloured reflections |

| Source | Approx. colour temperature |
|---|---|
| candle | ~1900 K |
| tungsten | ~3200 K |
| daylight, noon | ~5500 K |
| overcast / shade | ~6500-7500 K |

## 5. Colour grades and finishes

| Grade | Description |
|---|---|
| teal-and-orange | warm skin against cool shadows and backgrounds |
| bleach bypass | desaturated, high contrast, silvery |
| matte / lifted blacks | faded shadows, soft contrast |
| crushed blacks | deep shadows without detail, punchy |
| split-toned | warm highlights, cool shadows |
| pastel high-key | light, airy, low saturation |
| monochrome / duotone | one hue or two inks; state the hexes |
| film-negative look | soft contrast, warm skin, gentle highlight roll-off, fine grain |
| clean commercial | neutral white balance, high clarity, no grain |

## 6. Illustration media and styles

| Medium | Write |
|---|---|
| flat vector | flat fills, no gradients, consistent stroke |
| line art | uniform [1.5] px lines, no fill |
| gouache | opaque matte paint, visible brush edges |
| watercolour | transparent washes, paper texture, soft blooms |
| ink | brush or pen linework, hatching for shade |
| risograph | two or three spot inks, grain, slight misregistration |
| screen print | flat ink layers, halftone dots |
| linocut / woodcut | carved high-contrast shapes, rough edges |
| low-poly 3D | faceted geometry, flat shading |
| clay / plasticine look | soft rounded forms, fingerprints, studio light |
| 3D render | physically based materials, soft global illumination, subsurface scattering on skin or wax |
| isometric | 30° projection, no vanishing points |

Movements and eras (describe properties, never a living artist): Bauhaus (primary colours,
geometric forms, sans-serif), Swiss International Style (grid, asymmetric layout, objective
photography), Art Deco (symmetry, gold and black, stepped geometric ornament), Art Nouveau
(organic curves, floral ornament), Constructivism (diagonals, red and black, photomontage),
mid-century modern (organic shapes, muted teal, mustard and orange), Memphis (clashing pastels,
squiggles, terrazzo), ukiyo-e woodblock (flat colour areas, bold outlines, wave patterns),
pop art (Ben-Day dots, bold outlines, saturated primaries).

## 7. Materials and surfaces

brushed aluminium, anodised aluminium, polished chrome, matte ceramic glaze, frosted glass,
clear glass with caustics, satin plastic, soft-touch rubber, raw linen, boucle, walnut grain,
travertine, terrazzo, concrete with form-tie holes, kraft paper, uncoated card stock, wet
asphalt, corten steel. Name the finish (matte, satin, gloss) and the reflection you expect.
