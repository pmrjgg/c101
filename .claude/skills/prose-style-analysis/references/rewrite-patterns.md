# Rewrite patterns (before / after)

Each rewrite keeps the content and the voice; it changes how the sentence delivers it. Offer one
rewrite per finding; the author picks.

## 1. Filter words

| Pattern | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| saw/watched + clause | She watched the tide come in. | The tide came in. |
| heard + noun | He heard the gulls screaming. | Gulls screamed overhead. |
| felt + sensation | She felt the rope burn her palms. | The rope burned her palms. |
| realized/knew + fact | He realized the boat was gone. | The mooring was empty. |
| wondered + question | She wondered if he would come. | Would he come? |
| seemed/appeared | The house seemed empty. | Nothing moved in the house. (or keep if the doubt matters) |

## 2. Weak verbs and constructions

| Pattern | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| began/started to | He started to laugh. | He laughed. |
| was + -ing (no interruption) | She was walking to the dock. | She walked to the dock. |
| there was/were opener | There were three boats in the harbor. | Three boats rocked in the harbor. |
| it was X that | It was the smell that hit her first. | The smell hit her first. |
| nominalization | She made a decision to leave. | She decided to leave. |
| verb + adverb | He walked slowly and heavily. | He trudged. |
| could + perception | She could see the light. | The light showed through the reeds. |

## 3. Telling to showing

| Named state | Showing options (pick one, not all) |
|---|---|
| angry | a too-careful action; a short line; an object handled hard; silence where a reply belongs |
| afraid | distance kept; small tasks repeated; breath held; sentences shorten |
| sad | attention snagging on objects tied to the loss; a task abandoned halfway |
| happy | lightness in movement; generosity in small things; noticing beauty she usually ignores |
| nervous | over-preparation; repeating a question; fidgeting with a specific object |
| in love | tracking where the other person is in the room; rehearsed casualness |

Avoid swapping one stock gesture for a named emotion (clenched jaw, heart pounding, stomach
dropping); they are telling in body language. Prefer the gesture only this character would make.

## 4. Purple to plain

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The crimson sun bled its dying light across the vast, endless, shimmering sea. | The sun went down red over the water. |
| His voice was like velvet wrapped around steel, like honey poured over gravel. | His voice was soft and did not leave room for argument. |
| Tears cascaded like a waterfall down her porcelain cheeks. | She was crying and didn't wipe it away. |

## 5. Generic to specific

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The room was filled with an eerie silence. | The fridge clicked off, and then there was nothing. |
| She took in her surroundings. | (cut, or name the one thing she looks at) |
| Something shifted between them. | He handed her the oar without being asked. |
| Little did she know, this was only the beginning. | (cut; let the next chapter do it) |

## 6. Dialogue tags

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Get out," he growled menacingly. | "Get out." He didn't raise his voice. |
| "I'm fine," she smiled. | "I'm fine." She smiled. |
| "Really?" she queried. | "Really?" |
| "We have to go," he said urgently. | "We have to go. Now." |

## 7. Rhythm

Monotone run (all 10-14 words):
> She walked to the end of the dock in the rain. The boat was tied where Tomas had left it. She
> checked the knot twice and then checked it again. The water was higher than it had been all week.

Varied:
> She walked to the end of the dock in the rain. The boat was where Tomas had left it, tied with
> the knot their father taught them, the one that held until you wanted it not to. She checked it.
> Twice. The water was higher than it had been all week.

## 8. Simplification (grade 10 to grade 6)

Before (FK ~10.5):
> Notwithstanding the deteriorating conditions, Mara persisted in her determination to complete
> the crossing, acutely conscious that the surveyor's presence represented an irrevocable threat.

After (FK ~5.8, same content, same character):
> The weather was getting worse. Mara kept going anyway. She had to finish the crossing. And she
> knew the man in the back of her boat could take the marsh from them for good.
